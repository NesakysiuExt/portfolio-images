Privacy Policy

Application: NESAKYSIU business card (package lt.vilniustech.gsk.mkdf_23_s.emilijachorsevaite)
Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. Summary

This app is a digital business card with a CV and a project portfolio. It does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. All data you add stays on your device.

2. Data stored on your device

The app saves the following locally on the device, using Android Preferences DataStore and the app's private storage:

  • CV entries and portfolio projects that you add yourself, including their titles, descriptions and pictures you choose;
  • a card photo, if you take one with the camera;
  • the language you choose.

This data is never sent to the developer. You can remove the card photo in the Settings screen. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

3. Camera

The app opens the camera app of your phone when you choose to take a photo for the card. The photo is saved only in the app's private storage. The app does not upload it.

4. Contact form

When you send the contact form, the app opens your own email app with a ready message addressed to the developer. The message is sent only when you press Send in your email app. The name, contact and message you type are included in that email. The app itself does not transmit them.

5. Internet access

The app needs internet access only to load portfolio pictures from a public image repository on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). Loading a picture may reveal your IP address to GitHub, as with any web request. The app does not send any other data over the internet.

6. Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page and the date above will be updated.
